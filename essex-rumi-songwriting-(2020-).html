Essex Rumi songwriting (2020-)

Introduction

Essex Rumi is an English/Hindi hip-hop and electronic music project I started in 2020, drawing on my poetry, and songwriting ideas that emerged in the previous year. It deals with personal, often introspective themes mixed with political commentary. So far, I have written, composed, produced, and engineered each track on my own (out of necessity, for better or worse). Given here are the lyrics to each track released under the artist name, in descending order of year of release. The lyrics are also available to read on Essex Rumi’s Genius page and the tracks can be found on my Spotify artist page and my YouTube channel.
