Poetry (2016-)

Introduction

My first considerable brush with poetry was at around eight or nine years of age, with a free verse piece I wrote for an online poetry community, but I did not pursue the hobby seriously until I was fifteen, when it became my primary method to cope with and channel intense thoughts and emotions. Written here is a select section of my work in the medium starting from what I wrote then, in descending order of year of writing.

Another Day, Another Time (2019)

It’s easy to do your job all fine
Get yourself a raise a noon
You go home, drink a glass of wine
But there’s a thousand things to do
But all the clocks approach midnight
As He sounds the bells of doom
That’s the life you’ve got to live
For being you

It’s easy to pick up the telephone
Call a friend you’re meeting soon
You tell them things don’t seem alright
And they say they feel so too
And all the faith you had, at once
Leaves crashing through the roof
That’s the life you’ve got to live
For being you

It’s easy to let go of everything
When your head splits into two
You’re dazed and wrapped in delusion
“Surely, this could not be true”
But all the sanity that’s left
Declares you’ve gone cuckoo
That’s the life you’ve got to live
For being you.

Eyes (2018)

I open wide my eyes.
Gazing upon the air,
The living seem to die.

I swiftly shut my eyes.
Drawn bold into pitch black,
Their demise comes alive.

Knife (2018)

Holding a knife in your hand,
Stand in front of a mirror.
Point the blade at your image.
Do ask yourself for consent,
Lest it turns into murder.
When you feel like losing it,
Stab the image in the heart.
Run your hands over the cracks;
Wear them with pride. Love yourself.

Blur (2018)

Monday was a blur
Tuesday was absurd and
Wednesday could’ve been better
And Thursday was a burden

On Friday I broke down
On Saturday I’m crying
Sunday, I’m in bed
I look at myself dying

If I told myself it’d all be fine tomorrow
I’d live to hope I’m wrong.

Wings (2017)

A friend once told me of a male
Whose life was dull and oft in vain
Until, one night, amidst the black
A violent pain surged through his back

Looking to the mirror, he saw
A bump or bruise begin to form
Further into the night, confused
He felt his spine split into two

As darkness succumbed to morning
He woke up wrapped in crimson sheets
A bit of bone’d torn through the flesh
His back grew numb, the pain suppressed

The light gave way to dark unseen
A strange feeling began to creep
The male, for all the hurt it took
Could not resist the urge to look

But in the mirror, broken cold
Stood a grotesque of tangled bone
Around the pink, there’d grown a skin
Stretched lifeless, nearly paper-thin

At dawn came contempt and disgust
A sense of wonder followed dusk
Stroking his newfound pair of wings
The male now cried in vanity

Next day, while he was still in bed
He tried to choke himself to death
The wings began to flutter wild
His face, now blue, let out a smile

The man had died, the body’d killed
The wings spread out devoid of guilt
A splash of blood, a test of might
The bird had now begun its flight.

Guitar (2017)

I have a guitar at my place
It isn’t mine, but takes no space
The strings are loose, the board’s abused
But somehow it sounds like it should

The owner came in yesterday
To tell me it didn’t look the same
He blamed me for the mess I’d made
I told him, no, I couldn’t play

If I stand close and take a look
I see a face on polished wood
The skin is loose, the lips abused
But it feels just the way it should

For now, the guitar has no home
It stays with me, yet stays alone
But when I’ve cleaned the mess I’ve made
I’ll sit down, play it to my grave.

Mind (2017)

I have, indeed, lost my mind.
It should not be surprising,
Considering the fact that
It’s been on my mind for weeks.

A textbook case of drainage
Of the brain through the nostrils
Seems to be behind the loss.
An old trick, but effective.

Right here is where I first felt
A lack of nervous tissue—
Never mind. It’s still with me.
Must’ve lost my mind again.

River (2017)

I see a river flow.
I ask it where it goes.
I receive no response.

I run my hand along.
I feel it moving strong.
I asked the wrong question.

Ears (2017)

I once heard a distinct noise,
The kind that brings distraction.
I looked outside my window;
The noise was a man’s, in white.

I walked outside to ask him
What the noise was all about.
The man did not respond, but
I could see him take it in.

A victim of distraction,
I wanted the noise to stop.
As I began to speak so,
I noticed the man lacked ears.

Figuring the man could read,
I wrote on a paper sheet,
“Must you really make this noise
To bring to us distraction?”

The man read what I’d written
And seemed to smile a little.
He took the pen I wrote with,
And replied in soft cursive,

“I too had been distracted
By the noise that plagues us all.
Since I knew it would not stop,
I cut my ears off my head.

“Would you be interested
In buying, fifty apiece?
They are old, but sensitive;
They ought to fit your sockets.”

The idea was marvellous.
I went back home, where I’d been,
Collected what I needed,
And hurried back to the man.

Handing over my own ears,
I wrote another message,
“Here is my pair, freshly cut.
We could sell them together.”

I have not heard the noise since,
It brings me no distraction.
Would you like to buy our ears?
Mine are young; sixty apiece.

Acquaintance (2017)

Today, an old acquaintance caught my gaze.
Acknowledging my gaze, he stared back,
His eyes glowing cheerful with terror.

He tried, but couldn’t hide the disgust.
After all, I knew him as well as myself;
And yet, I could not remember his name.

Inches away, my breath condensed on the glass.
The guilt began to crawl under my skin,
As the intimacy between us rang hollow.

Extending my arm with a firm hand,
I hoped to set things right for the last time.
The blood stained the floor below.

Once again, I could feel the shame separate us.
The harder I tried to polish the mirror,
The clearer the cracks between us became.

Four (2017)

Sweating ashes
Never ready
Put on glasses
Head feels heavy
Self delusion
Red eyes run dry
Sight distortion
Tears won't comply

I am nothing
Feeling sorry
Reach out to sing
Windows block me
Scratch head in daze
Help but notice
Waves sync in phase
I control this

Holding my reins
Pulling southward
Wake up in vain
Life is absurd
Doubt did not die
All-consuming
Think but don't try
Wisdom blooming

Skin peeling off
Dirty doormat
Sour throat and cough
Brain in a vat
Restrict myself
Free in safekeep
All in your head
Go back to sleep.

Poetry (2017)

Ruled paper, pen in hand
Scribbles all over the sheet
Words that barely rhyme
No symbolism to be found
Would this be called poetry?

Sloppy artificial intelligence
Crawls through written works
An unordered list of words
Thrown together, gibberish
Would this be called poetry?

Paintbrush in hand, no paint
Air flows across the canvas
The fabric bends quite slightly
Not the same as purchased
Would this be called poetry?

Broken piano plays a note
Sustained for hours, days
The performer loses himself
Applause, acclaim, accolades
Would this be called poetry?

Life, death, all that is absurd
Sunshine seen for half a year
Loved ones dead in their arms
Curtains fresh out of laundry
Would this be called poetry?

Half the world at consensus
Critics proud of their lives
Museums and classes alike
It is as true as it could be
This would be called poetry.

Enemy (2017)

Every entry sees regret
Men ebb gently, tested well
Feet smell trenches, legs swell clenched
Feel deeds felt spell hell’s wet depths.

Apathy (2016)

Award thy acts that fly as ash
As wars at dawn halt dry?
Nay, anarchy always stars away
As man acts amply sly
Hands stank as tar at all afar
Ajax had lay a cry
All wars past, and wars that apply
All arts man arms as wry.

Listen (2016)

Listen closely,
And you’ll hear
The trees are talking,
The message clear

The wind blows,
The leaves, they fly
It’s all controlled,
It’s all compliance

The mist is fading,
The sky turns green
The moon makes way,
For a sun unseen

And the ground will split,
Under rolling grass
The fires will roar,
In a cosmic dance

The pain paves way,
For virtual bliss
Illusions real,
Our dreams amiss

And the sun will shine,
The fires enraged
The leaves will smile,
In clear restraint

And the sun will shine,
As leaves are dragged
Through fading mist,
Into the cracks

The dance is set,
Foretold in plain
An endless sun,
An endless rain.

The Machine (2016)

The machine is immortal
It towers above all
Leaving what is below
In endless shadow

It spreads far and wide
From the river to the mine
Watching the weak bend
The hunger never ends

The wheels keep turning
No weight heavy enough
To stop the giant cogs
That turn through the fog

He watched it day and night
Waiting for the time
His people would be free
An end to slavery

When would the time come?
The wait was futile
The only option, he felt
Was to bring change himself

Close ones rejected
Others tried to repress
The machine, they said
Would never come to an end

The people had been tricked
But his will, it was strong
In the darkest hour of night
The machine would be set alight!

He set out, untouched
Knowing their eyes watched
Every step he came to take
Wishing for a brighter day

He sneaked through the fog
Into the machine, at last
A tall tower that fed on fears
A hollow tube with endless gears

It was then that he could see
The heart of the machine
A thousand people operating
A million people under strings

He had met his nightmare
The machine had taken control
The people were mere slaves
All hope was set ablaze

He knew they wished an end
For they were human too
He looked into their eyes
It was clear, the despise

He threw his hands in defeat
The people, they understood
They tore the strings and broke free
It couldn't have been trickery!

Could he have won the fight?
Wondered he who realized
The strong mob ran, it gathered
Around him, now shattered

The emotion had numbed his pain
The blood, it ran across the floor
As the people dug into his flesh
He was left to bleed to death

The rain washed away the blood
No stains allowed to remain
A dying man had lost the fight
A dying man now realized

The machine, it fed on men
The men fed on the machine
People tied themselves to strings
They wanted what the machine brings

The people did not want freedom
They wanted to be slaves
The ones who fought against
Their efforts were in vain

The machine, it stays immortal
As it towers above all
Spreading far and wide
Inside the human mind.
